# Hi, I'm Ken 👋

Garage hacker in the SF Bay Area. I'm the kind of person who buys a car partly so I can take the battery apart, and whose Raspberry Pis outnumber the humans in the house. If it has a plug, an antenna, or an API, I will eventually tinker with it.

## 🔧 What I tinker with

**⚡ EVs & cars**
- Wrenching on a Fiat 500e — including high-voltage battery pack surgery (carefully!)
- ECU diagnostics with Multiecuscan, because the check engine light doesn't get the last word
- 🏎️ Occasional karting sessions to remind myself what "throttle response" is supposed to feel like

**📡 Radio & RF**
- Running an ADS-B flight-tracking feeder (PiAware on a Raspberry Pi) — the sky is my dashboard
- Slowly acquiring more antennas than any reasonable person needs

**🏠 Smart home & homelab**
- Home Assistant power user — if it's in my house, it's automated (or on the list)
- Linux-based Plex server, self-hosting, and the usual homelab rabbit holes
- Voice assistants wired into everything, whether they like it or not

**📈 Markets & data**
- Poking at OpenBB for open-source investment research
- Graphing my own utility bills because "why is the power bill high" deserves a chart, not a shrug

**🤖 AI-assisted tinkering**
- Building and debugging with Claude, Cursor, Copilot & friends — the robots and I get along fine
